# Solar-Panel Cleaning Mechanism — ISDC 2026 (1st Place)

Designed by me for Team Rotor FPV's autonomous solar-panel cleaning drone, which won **1st place at the International Space Drone Challenge 2026**.

## How it works
A servo drives a gear that meshes with a geared slider. This rack-and-pinion arrangement turns the servo's rotation into straight-line travel of the brush head, so the drone can move the brush against the panel surface.

## Assembly
- Servo motor on a dedicated servo mount
- Drive gear and geared slider (rack and pinion)
- Brush-and-ball cleaning head
- Three-part housing: bottom, middle and top covers
- Mounting plate and socket for attaching to the drone
