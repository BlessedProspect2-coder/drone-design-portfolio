# Swayatt — Aerothon 2026 Competition Frame

My custom airframe design for Team Rotor FPV's entry in **Aerothon 2026**. I designed the frame from scratch, along with a winch mechanism that lowers payloads on a rope.

## Assembly
- **Airframe:** custom top plate, top casing, holders and 35 mm standoffs
- **Payload winch:** drum pulley, rope, rope guider, rotor shaft and pulley mechanism, enclosed by a drop-mechanism cover
- **Avionics fitted for packaging:** Holybro Here3 GPS and RadioMaster RP4TD ELRS receiver (vendor models, included to check fit)
